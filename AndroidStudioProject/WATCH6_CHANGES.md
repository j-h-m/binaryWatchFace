# Galaxy Watch6 44 mm binary health face

- 480x480 WFF scene, six BCD columns, corrected arithmetic for each bit.
- Heart rate, date, steps and battery readouts.
- Existing manifest and Gradle SDK values retained (minSdk 36, targetSdk 37).
- Not compiled or tested on a watch; verify WFF data-source support for STEP_COUNT and BATTERY_PERCENT and test in Android Studio.
- Ambient-specific styling and LED glow are future enhancements.
